About Me

Every project I create teaches me something new and motivates me to improve further.

Travelling helps me discover different cultures, gain new perspectives, and stay motivated throughout my learning journey. I also have a keen interest in technology, artificial intelligence, and the ever-evolving world of software development. In my free time, I enjoy photography, watching science fiction and thriller series, and learning about space and the universe. I believe that curiosity, consistency, and a willingness to learn are the key qualities that help anyone grow both personally and professionally.
